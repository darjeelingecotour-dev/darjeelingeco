## Overview

Find your peace in the picturesque town of Pelling, tucked away in the lush landscapes of West Sikkim. Our Pelling Retreat is a rejuvenating 4-day escape designed to bring you close to nature and Sikkimese heritage. Offering some of the most unobstructed and awe-inspiring views of the Kanchenjunga mountain range, Pelling is a haven for those seeking tranquility away from crowded tourist hubs.

This journey takes you through ancient ruins, serene monasteries, cascading waterfalls, and sacred lakes. The blend of spiritual history and breathtaking natural beauty makes Pelling a truly mystical destination. Experience the thrill of walking on India's first glass skywalk and immerse yourself in the deep-rooted Buddhist culture that permeates this mountain retreat.

## Day-by-Day Itinerary

### Day 1: The Journey to Tranquility
* **Morning/Afternoon:** Arrive at Bagdogra Airport or NJP Railway Station. Meet your driver and begin the picturesque drive to Pelling via the river town of Jorethang. The journey follows winding mountain roads lined with lush greenery.
* **Evening:** Arrive in Pelling and check into your hotel. Spend a quiet evening admiring the sunset hues over the mountain peaks from your balcony.

### Day 2: History & Sky-High Views
* **Morning:** Start your day with a visit to the Pemayangtse Monastery, one of the oldest and most significant monasteries in Sikkim. Proceed to the Pelling Skywalk and marvel at the massive Chenrezig statue while enjoying thrilling views through the glass floor.
* **Afternoon:** Explore the Rabdentse Ruins, the former capital of Sikkim, nestled in a dense forest. Follow this with a visit to the impressive Kanchenjunga Falls.
* **Evening:** Return to the hotel. Enjoy a warm traditional Sikkimese dinner.

### Day 3: Sacred Waters & Waterfalls
* **Morning:** Drive to the deeply revered Khecheopalri Lake, known as the "Wishing Lake." It is believed that birds keep the lake's surface completely clear of leaves.
* **Afternoon:** Visit the Rimbi Waterfalls and the spectacular Changey Falls. Enjoy the lush surroundings and the sound of cascading water.
* **Evening:** Head to a local sunset point to witness the final rays of the sun illuminating the Kanchenjunga range before enjoying your final night in Pelling.

### Day 4: Departure
* **Morning:** Enjoy a relaxed breakfast amidst the mountains. Complete check-out formalities.
* **Afternoon:** Drive back to NJP Railway Station or Bagdogra Airport, carrying profound memories of your serene Pelling retreat.

## Packing List

* Comfortable walking shoes for exploring ruins and monastery grounds
* Warm clothing, especially for early mornings and evenings
* A good camera or smartphone with ample storage for landscape photography
* Refillable water bottle
* Motion sickness pills for the winding roads

## Local Tips

* **Monastery Etiquette:** Always walk clockwise around stupas and prayer wheels. Remove your shoes before entering the main prayer halls.
* **Skywalk Timing:** Visit the Skywalk early in the morning to avoid crowds and get the clearest photos.
* **Local Cuisine:** Don't miss trying local dishes like Thukpa, Gundruk, and traditional Sikkimese momos.

## Best Time to Visit

* **March to May:** Pleasant weather, clear skies, and beautiful blooming flowers.
* **September to November:** Post-monsoon clarity offers the absolute best and sharpest views of the Himalayan peaks.
