## Overview

The Sikkim Grand Circuit is our most comprehensive itinerary, designed to showcase the incredible diversity of this Himalayan state. Over 7 days, you'll transition from the bustling, vibrant streets of Gangtok to the serene, spiritual enclaves of South and West Sikkim. This tour is a vibrant tapestry of high-altitude glacial lakes, colossal statues, ancient monasteries, and panoramic mountain vistas.

Experience the thrill of ascending to the pristine Tsomgo Lake near the Indo-China border, find inner peace at the majestic Buddha Park in Ravangla, and marvel at the architectural wonders in Namchi. Concluding in the tranquil town of Pelling, this grand tour offers a complete immersion into the culture, spirituality, and natural grandeur of Sikkim.

## Day-by-Day Itinerary

### Day 1: Welcome to Gangtok
* **Morning/Afternoon:** Arrive at Bagdogra Airport/NJP Station. Drive to Gangtok, the capital of Sikkim, nestled along a steep mountain ridge.
* **Evening:** Check-in to your hotel. Take a leisurely stroll along MG Marg, India's first litter and spit-free zone, lined with cafes and shops.

### Day 2: The Glacial Lake Excursion
* **Morning:** Embark on an exciting drive to Tsomgo Lake (Changu Lake), situated at 12,400 ft. Enjoy the stunning azure waters surrounded by rugged mountains.
* **Afternoon:** Visit the legendary Baba Harbhajan Singh Mandir. (Optional: Nathula Pass visit, subject to permit availability).
* **Evening:** Return to Gangtok. Relax at your hotel or explore local eateries.

### Day 3: Gangtok Sightseeing & Drive to South Sikkim
* **Morning:** Visit the Enchey Monastery, Flower Exhibition Centre, and the Namgyal Institute of Tibetology.
* **Afternoon:** Drive towards South Sikkim, reaching the town of Namchi. Check into your hotel.
* **Evening:** Relax and enjoy the quieter pace of South Sikkim.

### Day 4: Spiritual South Sikkim
* **Morning:** Visit Siddhesvara Dham (Chardham) in Namchi, featuring a towering 87-foot statue of Lord Shiva. Proceed to Samdruptse Hill to view the magnificent Guru Padmasambhava statue.
* **Afternoon:** Drive to Ravangla. Visit the awe-inspiring Buddha Park (Tathagata Tsal) set against the backdrop of snow-capped peaks.
* **Evening:** Continue the drive to Pelling in West Sikkim. Check-in to your hotel.

### Day 5: Discovering Pelling
* **Morning:** Visit the Pemayangtse Monastery and walk across the thrilling Pelling Glass Skywalk.
* **Afternoon:** Explore the Rabdentse Ruins and the thunderous Kanchenjunga Falls.
* **Evening:** Enjoy a quiet evening in Pelling with dinner at your hotel.

### Day 6: Pelling's Hidden Gems
* **Morning:** Visit the sacred Khecheopalri Lake and Rimbi Waterfalls.
* **Afternoon:** Enjoy a scenic village walk or visit a local organic farm to understand Sikkimese agriculture.
* **Evening:** Final sunset viewing and a farewell dinner.

### Day 7: Journey Home
* **Morning:** Breakfast at the hotel.
* **Afternoon:** Check-out and drive back to Bagdogra/NJP with incredible memories of your Grand Sikkim Circuit.

## Packing List

* Heavy woolens (jackets, thermals, gloves, beanies) for Tsomgo Lake
* Comfortable everyday walking shoes and trekking shoes
* Sunglasses and high SPF sunscreen (essential for high altitudes)
* Inner Line Permits require passport-size photos and original ID proofs (carry multiple copies)

## Local Tips

* **Permits:** Tsomgo Lake and Baba Mandir require special permits. Provide your documents to your tour operator a day in advance.
* **Altitude Sickness:** Stay hydrated and avoid heavy meals before ascending to Tsomgo Lake to prevent altitude sickness.
* **MG Marg:** Vehicles are not allowed on MG Marg; prepare for some walking in Gangtok.

## Best Time to Visit

* **March to May:** Pleasant weather, ideal for sightseeing across all regions.
* **October to mid-December:** Clear skies for panoramic mountain views, though Tsomgo Lake will be quite cold.
