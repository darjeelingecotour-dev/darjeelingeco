## Overview

Embark on a timeless journey through the "Queen of the Hills" with our Darjeeling Classic tour. This meticulously crafted 5-day itinerary offers the perfect blend of natural splendor, colonial heritage, and vibrant local culture. From the world-renowned tea estates draping the emerald hillsides to the majestic views of the Kanchenjunga range, every moment in Darjeeling is straight out of a postcard.

Immerse yourself in the enchanting atmosphere as you ride the historic Darjeeling Himalayan Railway, witness breathtaking sunrises, and explore serene Buddhist monasteries. Whether you're a nature lover, a photography enthusiast, or simply seeking a peaceful retreat, this classic tour promises an unforgettable Himalayan experience.

## Day-by-Day Itinerary

### Day 1: Arrival & The Scenic Ascent
* **Morning/Afternoon:** Arrive at NJP Railway Station or Bagdogra Airport. Board your private vehicle for a scenic drive to Darjeeling via Kurseong. Enjoy the winding roads and changing landscapes as you ascend into the hills.
* **Evening:** Check-in to your hotel. Spend a relaxed evening strolling along the famous Mall Road (Chowrasta) and soaking in the cool mountain breeze.

### Day 2: Sunrise & Spiritual Serenity
* **Morning:** Wake up early (around 4:00 AM) for a drive to Tiger Hill. Witness a spectacular sunrise over Mt. Kanchenjunga, with the peaks turning golden. On the way back, visit the Batasia Loop and the historic Ghoom Monastery.
* **Afternoon:** After a hearty breakfast, visit the Japanese Peace Pagoda, offering panoramic views of the town and surrounding mountains.
* **Evening:** Free time for personal exploration or relaxing at a traditional tea lounge.

### Day 3: Heritage & Wildlife
* **Morning:** Visit the Himalayan Mountaineering Institute and the Padmaja Naidu Himalayan Zoological Park, home to rare species like the Red Panda and Snow Leopard.
* **Afternoon:** Head to the Happy Valley Tea Estate (or similar). Learn about the tea-making process and sample some of the world's finest brews.
* **Evening:** Explore local markets to pick up souvenirs, exquisite tea blends, and traditional Tibetan handicrafts.

### Day 4: Toy Trains & Cascades
* **Morning:** Experience a magical joyride on the UNESCO World Heritage Darjeeling Himalayan Railway (Toy Train).
* **Afternoon:** Drive down to the Rock Garden and Ganga Maya Park. Enjoy the terraced gardens, beautiful waterfalls, and tranquil surroundings.
* **Evening:** Last-minute shopping and a farewell dinner at a popular local restaurant.

### Day 5: Farewell to the Hills
* **Morning:** Enjoy a leisurely breakfast with mountain views. Check-out from your hotel.
* **Afternoon:** Drive back to NJP Railway Station or Bagdogra Airport with beautiful memories of your Darjeeling trip.

## Packing List

* Warm layers (fleece jackets, sweaters) as evenings can be chilly year-round
* Comfortable walking shoes or trekking boots
* Raincoat or umbrella (weather can be unpredictable)
* Sunscreen, sunglasses, and a wide-brimmed hat
* Personal medications and basic first-aid kit

## Local Tips

* **Tea Tasting:** Always buy Darjeeling tea from certified estates or reputed stores like Nathmulls or Golden Tips.
* **Toy Train Tickets:** Book the Toy Train joyride well in advance, especially during peak tourist seasons.
* **Early Mornings:** Tiger Hill gets very crowded; leave earlier than your driver suggests to secure a good viewing spot.
* **Respect Local Culture:** Dress modestly when visiting monasteries and temples.

## Best Time to Visit

* **Spring (March to May):** Pleasant weather, clear skies, and blooming rhododendrons.
* **Autumn (October to November):** Crisp air, excellent visibility of the Kanchenjunga peaks, and vibrant festival vibes.
