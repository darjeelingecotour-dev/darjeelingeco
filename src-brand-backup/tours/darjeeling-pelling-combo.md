## Overview

Experience the perfect Himalayan harmony with our Darjeeling & Pelling Combo tour. This meticulously balanced 7-day itinerary seamlessly weaves together the colonial charm of West Bengal's premier hill station with the deeply spiritual and naturally pristine environment of West Sikkim. 

Spend your first three days exploring the world-famous tea gardens, heritage railways, and bustling markets of Darjeeling. Then, cross over into the tranquil state of Sikkim to spend three days in Pelling, where you'll walk amongst ancient monastic ruins, thunderous waterfalls, and a thrilling glass skywalk. Offering distinct cultures but equally spectacular views of the Kanchenjunga range, this combo tour provides a diverse and enriching mountain holiday.

## Day-by-Day Itinerary

### Day 1: Arrival in Darjeeling
* **Morning/Afternoon:** Arrive at Bagdogra/NJP. Embark on a scenic drive up the winding roads to Darjeeling.
* **Evening:** Check-in to your hotel. Relax and take an evening stroll along the famous Chowrasta (Mall Road).

### Day 2: Darjeeling Sunrise & Sightseeing
* **Morning:** Early morning (4:00 AM) drive to Tiger Hill for a spectacular sunrise over Mt. Kanchenjunga. Visit Batasia Loop and Ghoom Monastery on the way back.
* **Afternoon:** Visit the Himalayan Mountaineering Institute, Padmaja Naidu Himalayan Zoological Park, and the serene Peace Pagoda.
* **Evening:** Explore local tea boutiques and enjoy the cool mountain evening.

### Day 3: Tea Gardens & Toy Train
* **Morning:** Take the heritage Toy Train joyride (subject to booking/availability).
* **Afternoon:** Visit the Happy Valley Tea Estate to understand the art of tea processing. Proceed to the Rock Garden for a relaxing afternoon.
* **Evening:** Leisure time for shopping for Tibetan curios and local woolens.

### Day 4: Cross over to Pelling
* **Morning:** After breakfast, bid farewell to Darjeeling and drive towards Pelling in West Sikkim. The drive takes you through picturesque tea gardens and along the Teesta/Rangeet rivers.
* **Afternoon:** Arrive in Pelling and check-in to your hotel.
* **Evening:** Enjoy the peaceful, quiet ambiance of Pelling, a stark contrast to bustling Darjeeling.

### Day 5: Pelling Heritage & Skywalk
* **Morning:** Visit the grand Pemayangtse Monastery. Proceed to the Chenrezig statue and walk across the exhilarating Pelling Glass Skywalk.
* **Afternoon:** Hike through the forested path to explore the historic Rabdentse Ruins, the ancient capital of Sikkim, offering stunning valley views.
* **Evening:** Return to the hotel for a traditional Sikkimese dinner.

### Day 6: Nature & Waterfalls
* **Morning:** Drive to the sacred Khecheopalri Lake, nestled amidst a dense forest.
* **Afternoon:** Visit the powerful Kanchenjunga Falls and Rimbi Waterfalls.
* **Evening:** Relax at your hotel and prepare for departure the next day.

### Day 7: Journey Home
* **Morning:** Enjoy a final breakfast with views of the Himalayas. Check-out from your hotel.
* **Afternoon:** Scenic drive back to NJP/Bagdogra for your onward journey.

## Packing List

* Layered clothing, as temperatures vary between Darjeeling and Pelling.
* Good walking shoes for monastery visits and ruins exploration.
* Sun protection (sunglasses, hat, sunscreen).
* Camera/Binoculars for bird watching and mountain photography.

## Local Tips

* **Border Crossing:** Keep your ID proofs handy as you will cross state borders from West Bengal into Sikkim, requiring routine police check-post verification.
* **Pace Yourself:** The transition from Darjeeling to Pelling involves several hours of driving on winding roads; carry motion sickness medication if needed.
* **Shopping:** Buy your tea in Darjeeling, but save your souvenir shopping for authentic Buddhist artifacts and singing bowls in Sikkim.

## Best Time to Visit

* **March to May:** Bright, pleasant days with blooming flora across both regions.
* **October to mid-December:** The absolute best time for clear, uninterrupted views of the Kanchenjunga massif from both Darjeeling and Pelling.
