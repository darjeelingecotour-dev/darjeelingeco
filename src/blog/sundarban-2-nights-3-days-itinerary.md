## Why choose two nights and three days?

A two-night, three-day plan gives more time on the waterways than a rushed day trip. It is suitable for fixed departures, families and small groups when road transfer, boat operation and accommodation are coordinated as one written package.

## Day 1: Kolkata to Godkhali and resort transfer

Depart from the confirmed Kolkata pickup point and travel to the jetty. Board the assigned boat after local formalities, then continue toward the resort or island stay. The afternoon may include a short orientation cruise or village experience depending on tide and arrival time.

## Day 2: Full-day mangrove cruise

Use the principal day for regulated waterways and available watchtower routes. The route depends on permits, tide, weather and official instructions. Wildlife sightings, including tiger sightings, cannot be guaranteed.

## Day 3: Local experience and Kolkata return

Complete a short morning activity, then return by boat and road. Keep the evening flexible because river and road timing can vary.

## What should the price include?

A transparent fixed-departure quote should state Kolkata or Canning pickup, road vehicle type, boat sharing, resort category, room sharing, meals, guide, forest permits and taxes. Camera charges, premium menu requests, private-boat upgrades and foreign-national supplements should be identified before payment.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is a tiger sighting guaranteed?

No. The journey is through a natural habitat, and sightings depend on wildlife movement.

### Is the boat private?

Only when the quotation explicitly says private boat. Budget prices normally use shared or group operations.

### Are all watchtowers covered?

Routes are permit, tide and time dependent. No operator should guarantee every point regardless of conditions.
