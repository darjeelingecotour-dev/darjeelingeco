## Why permits are important

North Sikkim includes restricted and high-altitude routes. Travel generally requires documentation, authorized processing and an eligible local vehicle. Requirements can change, so the final document list must be reconfirmed close to travel rather than copied from an old post.

## Documents to prepare

Indian travellers should keep original government photo identification and clear photocopies, along with recent passport-size photographs if requested by the operator. Foreign-national requirements can differ and should be checked before any non-refundable booking.

## Route and vehicle conditions

Lachen, Lachung, Yumthang Valley and Gurudongmar Lake involve long mountain drives. Access can change because of weather, snow, landslides, administrative restrictions or road repairs. A package must never guarantee access to a restricted point regardless of official closure.

## High-altitude planning

Gurudongmar Lake is a very high-altitude excursion and may not be suitable for every traveller. Travellers with health concerns should obtain professional medical advice before booking. Children, older travellers and anyone reporting discomfort should not be pressured to continue upward.

## What the quotation should state

The written quotation should identify pickup and drop points, shared or private vehicle, accommodation standard, meal plan, permit processing, optional Zero Point charges and any government fees payable directly. A low shared-tour rate should not be presented as though it includes a private vehicle.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can a North Sikkim permit be guaranteed?

No. Approval and route access remain subject to the relevant authorities and current conditions.

### Is Aadhaar always sufficient?

Document acceptance can vary. Confirm the current requirement and carry another eligible original ID when advised.

### Is Zero Point included?

Only if explicitly listed. It is often optional and may be payable directly.

### What if a route closes?

The operator should explain the practical alternative and any cost difference before the traveller accepts it.
