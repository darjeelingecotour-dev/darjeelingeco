## Kashmir 3 nights 4 days itinerary

### Day 1: Srinagar arrival and local sightseeing

Pickup from Srinagar Airport, hotel check-in and a practical local circuit based on arrival time. The written plan may include selected Mughal Gardens and the Dal Lake area. A Shikara ride is included only when stated in the final quotation.

### Day 2: Gulmarg day excursion

Travel from Srinagar to Gulmarg and return. Snow, skiing, sledging, ATV services, chain vehicles and Gondola tickets are not automatically included. These services remain optional or payable directly unless clearly added to the quotation.

### Day 3: Pahalgam day excursion

Visit Pahalgam and the main town area. Union vehicles for Aru Valley, Betaab Valley or Chandanwari are separate unless explicitly included because local transport rules can apply.

### Day 4: Departure

Breakfast and transfer to Srinagar Airport with a suitable reporting-time buffer.

The indicative starting price is based on a minimum of four paying adults sharing two standard rooms, breakfast and dinner, and one private vehicle for the written Srinagar-to-Srinagar route. The final price changes with travel date, group size, hotel, room type and vehicle.
