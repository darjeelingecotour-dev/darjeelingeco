## Overview
Step into the pristine wilderness of Assam with our Kaziranga Wildlife Safari. A UNESCO World Heritage Site, Kaziranga National Park is globally renowned as the ultimate sanctuary for the Great Indian One-Horned Rhinoceros. Over four days, immerse yourself in a thriving ecosystem comprising dense tropical forests, tall elephant grass, and sprawling marshlands. 

Beyond the rhinos, the park is home to a staggering population of tigers, wild water buffaloes, Asian elephants, and an array of migratory and resident birds. This tour provides deeply immersive wildlife experiences through both elephant-back safaris at dawn and extensive jeep safaris, maximizing your chances of encountering the jungle's most elusive residents.

## Day-by-Day Itinerary

### Day 1: Arrival in Guwahati & Drive to Kaziranga
- **Morning:** Arrive at Lokpriya Gopinath Bordoloi International Airport (Guwahati). Our representative will meet you and initiate the scenic drive towards Kaziranga (approx. 4-5 hours).
- **Afternoon:** En route, enjoy the lush green landscapes and the mighty Brahmaputra river. Stop for a traditional Assamese thali lunch. 
- **Evening:** Check in to your eco-resort on the fringes of the national park. Relax by the bonfire, enjoy a briefing from our naturalist, and prepare for an early morning safari.

### Day 2: Central Range Elephant Safari & Western Range Jeep Safari
- **Morning:** Wake up before dawn for an unforgettable Elephant Safari in the Kohora (Central) Range. The elevated vantage point offers the best opportunity to spot rhinos grazing in the tall grass. Return to the resort for a hearty breakfast.
- **Afternoon:** After lunch, embark on a Jeep Safari in the Bagori (Western) Range, known for its high density of rhinos, wild buffalos, and swamp deer. 
- **Evening:** Visit a nearby tea garden or the Kaziranga Orchid and Biodiversity Park to witness the rich flora and traditional cultural performances.

### Day 3: Eastern Range Birding & Safari
- **Morning:** Head to the Agaratoli (Eastern) Range for a morning jeep safari. This zone is a paradise for bird watchers, with numerous water bodies attracting migratory birds like pelicans and bar-headed geese. You might also spot otters and turtles.
- **Afternoon:** Return to the resort for a leisurely lunch. Optional afternoon jeep safari in the Burapahar Range, known for its rugged terrain and chances to spot the Hoolock Gibbon and capped langur.
- **Evening:** Enjoy a quiet evening at the resort, sharing wildlife stories over a delicious dinner.

### Day 4: Departure to Guwahati
- **Morning:** Enjoy a relaxed breakfast amidst the sounds of nature. Take a final stroll around the resort grounds.
- **Afternoon:** Drive back to Guwahati. If time permits, visit the Kamakhya Temple before being dropped off at the airport or railway station for your onward journey.

## Packing List
- Lightweight, breathable clothing in neutral earth tones (khaki, olive, brown) to blend in with the surroundings.
- A light jacket or fleece for chilly early morning safaris.
- Sturdy walking shoes or hiking boots.
- Binoculars and a camera with a good zoom lens (300mm or more recommended).
- Sunscreen, sunglasses, and a wide-brimmed hat.
- Insect repellent and a personal first-aid kit.

## Local Tips
- **Silence is Golden:** Keep noise to a minimum during safaris to avoid startling the wildlife.
- **Respect Boundaries:** Never ask your driver to take the jeep off the designated tracks.
- **Try Local Delicacies:** Ask your resort for local Assamese dishes like *Masor Tenga* (sour fish curry) or *Khar*.

## Best Time to Visit
The park is open from **November to April**. The best time to visit is between **February and March** when the grass is burnt off, making wildlife spotting significantly easier, and the weather is pleasantly cool. (Note: The park remains closed during the monsoon season from May to October).
