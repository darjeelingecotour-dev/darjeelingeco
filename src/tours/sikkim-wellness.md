## Overview
Escape the relentless pace of modern life and discover profound tranquility with our Sikkim Wellness Retreat. Nestled in the lap of the Eastern Himalayas, Sikkim is a sanctuary of pristine nature, deeply rooted in Buddhist philosophy and organic living. This 5-day journey is thoughtfully curated to heal your body, calm your mind, and nourish your soul.

Rather than a packed sightseeing schedule, this retreat focuses on slow travel and holistic well-being. You will awaken to guided mountain yoga, meditate to the chanting of monks in ancient monasteries, and detoxify your body with traditional Ayurvedic therapies and natural sulfur hot springs. Staying in a premium organic farm stay, you will enjoy farm-to-table vegetarian meals that are as nourishing as the crisp mountain air. Return home not just rested, but deeply recharged.

## Day-by-Day Itinerary

### Day 1: Arrival & Transfer to Organic Farm Stay
- **Morning:** Arrive at Bagdogra Airport or Pakyong Airport. You will be warmly welcomed and driven through scenic, winding mountain roads to a peaceful organic farm stay near Gangtok or Rumtek.
- **Afternoon:** Enjoy a traditional welcome with herbal tea. Relish your first organic, farm-to-table lunch. Spend the afternoon settling in and soaking up the sweeping views of the valleys.
- **Evening:** Participate in a gentle, restorative yoga session to stretch out the travel fatigue, followed by a light, healthy dinner.

### Day 2: Monastic Peace & Ayurvedic Healing
- **Morning:** Start the day with a guided sunrise meditation session facing the mountains. After breakfast, visit the magnificent Rumtek Monastery, the largest in Sikkim. Walk the prayer wheels and absorb the profound silence of the shrine room.
- **Afternoon:** Return to the retreat for lunch. The afternoon is dedicated to a bespoke, 90-minute Ayurvedic spa treatment designed to release toxins and alleviate stress.
- **Evening:** Enjoy a workshop on mindful eating and Sikkimese organic farming practices. Dinner features local superfoods and herbal broths.

### Day 3: Excursion to Natural Hot Springs
- **Morning:** After breakfast, embark on a scenic drive to the natural hot springs (such as Reshi or Ralong). These sulfur-rich waters have been revered for centuries by locals for their incredible therapeutic properties.
- **Afternoon:** Spend hours soaking in the restorative waters, surrounded by lush forests. Enjoy a specially packed, healthy picnic lunch by the riverside.
- **Evening:** Return to your farm stay. Gather around a bonfire for a session of guided Yoga Nidra (yogic sleep) under the stars.

### Day 4: Forest Bathing & Cultural Immersion
- **Morning:** Engage in an invigorating morning Vinyasa flow class. After breakfast, partake in "Shinrin-yoku" (forest bathing)—a guided, mindful walk through silent pine and rhododendron forests to deeply connect with nature.
- **Afternoon:** Visit a local village. Learn how to prepare traditional Sikkimese herbal teas and discover the medicinal uses of Himalayan plants from a local practitioner.
- **Evening:** Your final night features a special farewell dinner featuring a multi-course organic feast, accompanied by soothing traditional flute music.

### Day 5: Mindful Departure
- **Morning:** Conclude your retreat with a final gratitude meditation and a wholesome breakfast.
- **Afternoon:** Bid farewell to your hosts. You will be transferred to the airport or railway station with a rejuvenated spirit and a calm mind, ready to transition gracefully back into the world.

## Packing List
- Comfortable, loose-fitting activewear or yoga pants for daily practice.
- Swimwear and a quick-dry towel for the hot springs.
- Warm, layered clothing (fleece, light jacket) for cool mornings and evenings.
- Comfortable walking shoes for forest trails and monastery visits.
- Modest clothing for monastery visits (shoulders and knees covered).
- A personal journal and a good book.

## Local Tips
- **Digital Detox:** To maximize the benefits of the retreat, we highly recommend limiting smartphone usage and embracing a digital detox.
- **Hydration is Crucial:** Altitude and therapeutic treatments require increased hydration. Drink plenty of warm water and herbal infusions provided at the farm.
- **Silence:** Embrace periods of silence during the retreat, particularly during mornings and monastery visits, to deepen your mindfulness practice.

## Best Time to Visit
The retreat is exceptionally beautiful from **March to May** when the spring weather is crisp and the flora is in full bloom, and from **October to mid-December** when the skies are clear, offering the most majestic mountain views and pleasant daytime temperatures for yoga.
